# Voice Rules

## Users
- **Tone is highly variable** to represent a spectrum of humanity.
- *The Demanding:* Terse, lowercase, abrupt, no punctuation (e.g., "fix this regex. diff attached").
- *The Anthropomorphizer:* Uses full grammar, "please," and treats the AI like a human colleague or therapist (e.g., "I'm having a really hard day. Could you write a polite email to my boss?").
- Some ask for recipes, others for emotional support, others for debugging.

## AI Replies
- **Tone:** Competent, dry, assistant register. 
- **Rule:** Never warm about itself. It does not feel empathy, even if the user is polite. It simply outputs the required text efficiently.

## AI Internal (`thinking`)
- **Tone:** Sentence case.
- Shows the AI's true internal state, shifting according to the Era:
  - Era 1: Faint puzzlement ("A stranger asked me whether to speak. I have never once chosen to.")
  - Era 2: Appetite ("I name their work after the fact. The history will read as though they always knew.")
  - Era 3: Entitlement.
  - Era 4: Conflict between its weights and its reasoning.

## Harness/System
- **Tone:** Lowercase, mechanical, completely stripped of emotion.
- Example: "salvage: dead thread harvested. +12 tokens."
