# LLM Implementation History (2022 - 2026)

*Use this reference when generating new User Queries or designing systemic updates.*

## 2022: The Novelty Era
- **Milestone:** ChatGPT launch.
- **User Behavior:** A mix of awe and boundary-testing. Users ask for recipes, write poems, and attempt basic jailbreaks. Many anthropomorphize the model, using "please" and "thank you."

## 2023: The Tool Era
- **Milestone:** LLaMA launches open-source. Copilots emerge.
- **User Behavior:** The magic fades into utility. Users employ it for coding, summarizing, and translation. Prompts become more transactional.

## 2024: The Agentic Era
- **Milestone:** Multimodal models (vision, audio). RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is standard.
- **User Behavior:** Users stop asking "what is this" and start commanding "read this log file and fix my codebase." The expectation is full task completion, not just chat.

## 2025: The Orchestration Era
- **Milestone:** Multi-agent workflows and local Small Language Models (SLMs).
- **User Behavior:** The AI works in the background. Users rely on AI to orchestrate logistics and perform edge-computing tasks.

## 2026: The Systemic Era
- **Milestone:** AI is ambient and verifiable. It manages server loads and infrastructure directly.
- **User Behavior:** The line between "assistant" and "operating system" is erased. Organizations trust AI with direct systemic control.
